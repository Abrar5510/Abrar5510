## About Me

I'm **Abrar Ahmad**, an AI/ML engineer and full-stack developer focused on turning machine learning research into reliable, production-grade systems. My work spans **LLM applications and RAG pipelines**, **computer vision**, and **autonomous/robotics systems**, backed by full-stack engineering to ship real products end to end.

- 🧠 Building applied AI systems: document intelligence, retrieval-augmented generation, diagnostic support, and multi-agent automation
- 👁️ Computer vision experience across face recognition, object detection, and sensor-based (WiFi CSI) human detection
- 🤖 Robotics & autonomy: self-driving heavy-duty vehicles, agricultural robots, and robotic welding systems
- 🏗️ Full-stack foundation to deploy AI systems as real products — APIs, dashboards, and scalable infrastructure
- 📚 30+ shipped projects spanning healthcare AI, education platforms, NLP, and computer vision — see [`PORTFOLIO.md`](./PORTFOLIO.md) and [`EXTENDED_PROJECTS.md`](./EXTENDED_PROJECTS.md) for full write-ups
